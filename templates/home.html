Process

How Antholytix AI Works

A seamless pipeline from food chemistry to instant digital freshness prediction.

01

Indicator Placement

A freshness indicator label made using natural anthocyanin pigments is placed inside or near the food packaging where it can respond to chemical changes during spoilage.

02

Chemical Colour Change

As the food begins to spoil, gases and pH variations cause the anthocyanin indicator to gradually change colour, reflecting the freshness condition of the food.

03

Scan Using Smartphone

The user opens the Antholytix AI platform and simply scans the indicator label using their smartphone camera.

04

AI Colour Analysis

The system processes the captured image and extracts colour data which is analysed using calibrated freshness ranges derived from chemical models.

05

Instant Freshness Result

Antholytix AI predicts whether the food is fresh, moderately fresh, or spoiled — helping users make safer consumption decisions instantly.

Chemistry FAQ

Understanding the Science

Antholytix AI is based on the principle that food spoilage causes chemical changes in the surrounding environment. As food begins to degrade, compounds such as organic acids, amines and other volatile substances are released, altering the pH conditions around the food. These chemical changes can be detected using natural pH-sensitive indicators.

The project uses anthocyanin pigments extracted from natural sources such as red cabbage. Anthocyanins are well-known natural indicators that change colour depending on the pH of their surroundings, making them ideal for detecting chemical changes associated with food spoilage.

During the spoilage process, microorganisms break down food components and release gases and acidic or basic compounds. These substances interact with the anthocyanin indicator, causing visible colour changes. By analysing the colour variation, the freshness stage of the food can be estimated accurately.

The chemistry concept forms the scientific foundation of the system, as the indicator colour change represents the chemical state of the food. The platform captures this colour using a smartphone camera and interprets the indicator condition to present a freshness prediction for the user.
