Core Technology

🧪

Chemical Indicators

Natural anthocyanin pigments extracted from plant sources act as pH indicators. As food begins to spoil, volatile compounds increase surrounding pH, causing visible colour changes in the indicator label.

🎨

AI Color Recognition

The mobile application captures colour values from the freshness indicator using the smartphone camera. The RGB data is converted to HSV space for accurate hue-based classification.

🤖

Freshness Classification

The system compares captured colour hue values with calibrated reference data to classify food freshness as Fresh, Caution, or Spoiled — based on the anthocyanin pH-colour map.

📦

Smart Food Monitoring

Antholytix technology enables future smart packaging systems where food freshness indicators are embedded directly into packaging materials for continuous monitoring.

Calibration

Anthocyanin Color Map

The detection system uses a calibrated pH-to-color lookup based on red cabbage anthocyanin behaviour.

H 0–30 / 330–360

Red / Pink → pH 1–4 → FRESH

Strongly acidic environment. Food is fresh and safe to consume.

H 270–330

Purple → pH 5–6 → FRESH

Slightly acidic. Food is still fresh and safe.

H 200–270

Blue → pH 7–8 → CAUTION

Neutral to slightly basic. Consume soon — spoilage beginning.

H 90–200

Green → pH 9–10 → SPOILED

Basic environment. Spoilage detected — ammonia forming.

H 45–90

Yellow → pH 11+ → SPOILED

Highly basic. Do not consume under any circumstances.
