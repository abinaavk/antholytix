Chemical Indicators

Natural anthocyanin pigments extracted from plant sources act as pH indicators. As food begins to spoil, volatile compounds increase surrounding pH, causing visible colour changes.

AI Color Recognition

The mobile application captures colour values from the freshness indicator using the smartphone camera and processes the RGB spectrum using trained models.

Machine Learning Model

The system compares captured colour values with calibrated reference data to classify food freshness as fresh, caution, or spoiled.

Smart Food Monitoring

Antholytix technology enables future smart packaging systems where food freshness indicators are embedded directly into packaging materials.

Why Use the Antholytix AI App?

🔬 Chemistry-Based Detection

The app interprets colour changes in natural anthocyanin indicators that react to chemical variations caused by food spoilage, providing a science-based freshness evaluation.

🧪 Real-Time Freshness Analysis

Instead of relying only on expiry dates, the system analyses the actual chemical environment around the food through indicator colour variations, giving a more realistic freshness insight.

📸 Smart Colour Recognition

Using the smartphone camera, the application captures the colour of the freshness indicator and compares it with calibrated freshness ranges derived from experimental observations.

🌿 Natural Indicator System

The technology is built around natural pH indicators such as anthocyanins extracted from plant sources, making the concept chemically reliable and environmentally friendly.

⚡ Instant Chemical Insight

Within seconds, the app converts the indicator colour data into a clear freshness prediction, helping users understand the chemical state of their food.

🍎 Reducing Food Waste

By monitoring chemical spoilage indicators instead of fixed expiry dates, the system encourages smarter consumption and helps reduce unnecessary food wastage.
