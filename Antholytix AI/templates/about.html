Purpose of Antholytix AI

Food spoilage is a global issue leading to health risks and large amounts of food waste. Antholytix AI introduces a smart freshness detection system where natural chemical indicators change colour as food spoils and artificial intelligence interprets those colour changes instantly using digital image analysis.

Preparing the Natural Indicator Label

Antholytix AI uses natural anthocyanin pigments extracted from red cabbage as a chemical freshness indicator. The colour of this pigment changes when food begins to spoil, allowing the label to visually signal freshness.

1. Extract the Pigment

Finely chop fresh red cabbage and boil it in distilled water for 10–15 minutes to extract the purple anthocyanin solution.

2. Filter the Extract

Filter the boiled mixture using filter paper or a fine cloth to separate the clear indicator solution from solid cabbage particles.

3. Prepare the Label Paper

Dip small pieces of filter paper or absorbent paper into the anthocyanin solution and allow them to dry completely.

4. Apply to Packaging

The dried indicator paper can be placed inside food packaging where it will change colour as chemical conditions change during spoilage.

5. Analyse with Antholytix AI

The smartphone camera captures the colour of the label and the system analyses the change to determine food freshness.

Chemistry in Everyday Life

Chemistry plays a vital role in our everyday lives, influencing the quality, safety and preservation of the food we consume. From natural pigments that change colour to indicate freshness, to the reactions that occur during food spoilage, chemical principles help us understand and monitor the substances around us. By applying chemistry in practical ways, innovations like Antholytix AI aim to promote healthier choices, improve food safety and encourage a deeper appreciation for the science that quietly supports our daily wellbeing.
