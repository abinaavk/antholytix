Understanding the Chemistry Behind Antholytix AI

Antholytix AI is based on the principle that food spoilage causes chemical changes in the surrounding environment. As food begins to degrade, compounds such as organic acids, amines and other volatile substances are released, altering the pH conditions around the food. These chemical changes can be detected using natural pH-sensitive indicators.

The project uses anthocyanin pigments extracted from natural sources such as red cabbage or similar plant materials. Anthocyanins are well known natural indicators that change colour depending on the pH of their surroundings, making them ideal for detecting chemical changes associated with food spoilage.

During the spoilage process, microorganisms break down food components and release gases and acidic or basic compounds. These substances interact with the anthocyanin indicator, causing visible colour changes. By analysing the colour variation, the freshness stage of the food can be estimated.

The chemistry concept forms the scientific foundation of the system, as the indicator colour change represents the chemical state of the food. The platform captures this colour using a smartphone camera and interprets the indicator condition to present a freshness prediction for the user.
